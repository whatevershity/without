Hi, my name is Adelaide, and a few years ago I took everything very literally.

Whenever I ran into someone I knew and, after a short chat about nothing, they said, “We should totally meet up!”, I actually believed we would. So I’d text them: “Hey, so what about that meetup?” And they’d reply: “I’d love to, but I don’t have time right now.” Later I’d ask again, and they’d say: “That’d be great, but not now.”

Eventually I realized that people just say these things. It wasn’t like I’d suggested something and they had to lie to spare my feelings. They started it, for no reason at all. It’s a lie built right into small talk.

Since then, I’ve been doing it too. I see an acquaintance, we spend two minutes chatting about nothing, and at the end, which I can hardly wait for, I say cheerfully: “We have to catch up! We really have to meet! It would be so nice to talk properly over coffee!” Then I walk away and laugh. Because, you know, I’m never actually going to call.
